# Activity Rotation Guide After the Kortz Center Heist Update

This guide describes a cautious activity rotation for players using replay or
no-save workflows after the Kortz Center Heist update. It is intended to prevent
excessive repetition of the same activity.

## General rule

Do not repeatedly launch the same heist or mission back-to-back. Return to normal
freemode play and alternate between different activity categories before replaying
an activity.

No rotation pattern guarantees that an account will avoid server-side checks.
Rockstar may change cooldowns or repetition rules in later updates.

## Cayo Perico

- Respect the full 144-minute cooldown.
- Once the cooldown has expired, do not immediately begin a chain of repeated
  Cayo Perico finales.
- Complete other activities between two Cayo Perico runs.
- Treat the end of the cooldown as permission to launch the heist again, not as a
  signal that repeated runs are safe.

## Other heists

Avoid repeatedly replaying the same finale, including:

- Apartment heists.
- The Doomsday Heist.
- The Diamond Casino Heist.
- The Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid.
- The Kortz Center Heist and other multi-stage robberies.

After completing one of these activities, switch to another category instead of
restarting the same finale immediately.

## Missions and shorter activities

The same moderation applies to contact missions, robberies, raids, and other
repeatable missions. Shorter duration does not make continuous repetition
advisable.

Useful activities to place between repeated heists or missions include:

- Freemode work or events.
- Business sales, sourcing, or management.
- Races, survivals, or adversary modes.
- Contact missions from a different mission chain.
- Preparations for a different heist.
- Regular exploration or non-reward-focused play.

## Example rotation

1. Complete one heist or finale.
2. Return to freemode.
3. Play activities from at least one different category.
4. Change to a different heist or mission chain.
5. Replay the original activity only after its cooldown and a genuine activity
   rotation.

Changing sessions or restarting the game is not a substitute for a cooldown and
does not by itself refresh server-side activity history.

## Practical reminder

- Avoid identical back-to-back completions.
- Alternate heists, missions, and freemode activities.
- Respect every displayed or known cooldown.
- Prefer varied, normal play over a fixed farming loop.
- Recheck current cooldown information after every GTA Online update.
